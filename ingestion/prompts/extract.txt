use remembered biology to add findings. Return null for absent fields.

assay coverage lists, example variants, literature, therapy tables and trial lists.

gene and partner must both be literal reported symbols, not inferred from biology.

not you, will normalize symbols. Do not invent HGNC IDs or cancer ontology IDs.

Copy quotes from the supplied text order. Do not reorder words to match an image.

the supplied page text. Preserve the exact raw diagnosis and specimen description.
